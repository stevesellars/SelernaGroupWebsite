Your travel kit

Keep these for the road.

The three takeaways from today, in one place. Start small, keep a person in charge of what goes out, and grow from there.

Your 90-day itinerary

  1. Week 1, unpack. List where your team’s time goes. Would anyone notice if you stopped?
  2. Week 2, leave it behind. Make a “stop doing” list: one report, one meeting, one process.
  3. Month 1, pilot one workflow. Write the job description first. Then pick the tool.
  4. Months 2–3, be the tour director. Check every output, teach it, and protect the time you get back.

Set up your own assistant

  1. Start a project with your instructions and files.
  2. Check the data settings so your work doesn’t train the model.
  3. Keep working in the project so it builds on past conversations.
  4. Connect email and calendar so it can see your week.
  5. Give an agent a goal, the tools it needs, and clear limits.
  6. Have it report back for your approval. Nothing goes out without you.

Packing rules for your data

  • Don’t pack what you wouldn’t email to a stranger: guest IDs, payments, private contracts.
  • Work data goes in work accounts, where you control who sees it and how it’s used.
  • A person approves anything that leaves the building.
  • Scammers use AI too. A request for money or a password? Confirm it another way.
